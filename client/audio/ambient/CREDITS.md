Local adaptations: short excerpts, mono downmix, 22,050 Hz / 16-bit PCM, 70 Hz high-pass filtering, crossfaded loop joins and gain adjustment with a 0.8 peak ceiling. `processing.json` records the original filenames, excerpt positions, loop lengths, gain and the SHA-256 of each bundled adaptation. Lava and fallback textures are synthesized in `client/js/soundscape.js`.

Uploaded clips are separate assets. Mapmakers must have permission to distribute their uploads with the game; these credits and CC0 terms apply only to the bundled recordings above.

The `campfire--38f4be41ad5842da9a02392e53ef3812f295d56072d7f737759b7b9f3980bd10.wav` library clip is the same PagDev adaptation imported through the Toolbox. It shares the campfire credit above.
