# The Lantern Road: quick test walkthrough

Open the [local walkthrough](http://127.0.0.1:3015/preview/walkthrough) alongside the game. Its **Visit NPC** and **Go to this marker** buttons move your connected player to named places. They never accept, inspect or complete a quest for you. Refresh the walkthrough after accepting a quest or choosing a route.

Player 2 continues your saved picnic and road progress. Player 1 has a separate save. Every new local session equips a level 100 avatar and level 100 golden sword, with faster movement, more health and reduced incoming damage. These boosts belong to the loopback fixture.

## Fast conversation and persistence check

1. Visit Mara and select **About Follow the Old Markers...** if that quest is already active. If it is new, ask **Adam is still saving places for the forest neighbours.**, then accept her request.
2. Ask **Why did you stop writing to Town?**. The popup shows your words under **You**, followed by Mara's answer under her name. She admits she mistook unanswered invitations for rejection.
3. Ask for directions. You get a named trail landmark and marker name, with no coordinates. Inspect the two markers using E or a click. A report is unavailable until both are checked.
4. Return to Mara and report what the arrows and knot showed. She recognises Rowan's knot and offers the relay investigation.
5. Open a fresh session from the walkthrough. The completed quest and inspected markers remain saved; Player 1 retains separate progress.
6. Use **Day**, **Night** and **Cycle** on the local walkthrough for ambience checks. Firefly brightness follows the shared clock. Forest shows leaves and pollen; coast shows spray and sand; Desert shows dust and wind; Lavaland shows embers and ash. Travel between areas: the clock and lighting should agree.

## Full story route

At each quest: ask the topic, optionally ask the follow-up question, accept, inspect the listed markers, then return to the NPC and report or choose a response. Completed picnic saves begin with the coast or forest leads. Chapters 2 and 3 can run in either order; Vince needs both finished reports.

### 1. The Lantern Picnic

1. **Adam → Bstrat → Adam:** sort out the borrowed basket. Return it or share it; Adam remembers that solution.
2. **Town Watch:** defeat three rats, then report that the path is clear. Earlier rat kills count.
3. **Bstrat:** choose quiet or music. She invites the watch; later greetings and the long table remember the choice.

## Things to check at the long table

- Quiet/music, public/private remembrance, caravan route and Rowan's shared watch/rest choice appear in **Do you remember how we got here?**
- Arranging relief lets the watch stay all evening. Family messages stay private. Carrying Will's personal invitation makes room for Jimi. The optional precision tool adds a golden lantern.
- Repairs, markers and choices remain personal to the avatar. Another player does not receive your quest completions or private reactions.
- No story conversation, quest direction or walkthrough exposes map coordinates. Normal map rendering can draw players and objects over the picnic scenery.


## Test NPC schedules and buildings

Open the local walkthrough and expand **Test daily NPC routines**. Time buttons freeze the shared world clock, so the HUD, lighting, ambient effects and NPCs all use the selected time. **Cycle** resumes normal time. **Visit** follows the NPC's current position, including indoors; the entrance buttons place you outside a named building so you can follow someone through the door yourself.

1. Set **14:00**, visit Adam and watch his market rounds.
2. Set **19:00**, go to the guesthouse entrance east of the market, and watch Adam or Bstrat walk through the door. Walk through it yourself to find them inside.
3. Set **22:00**. Sleeping neighbours stop greeting passersby. Talk to one and ask **What does your day usually look like?**; they explain being woken and their daily routine. Quest conversations still work indoors.
4. Set **08:00** to watch Adam leave and the watch return to the town hall. Set **14:00** to bring all three back outside.
5. Finish or replay the picnic while they are indoors. They come outside through their doors, join the picnic, then resume the activity for the current time.

Allow time for walking after changing the clock. A conversation can pause an NPC for twenty seconds, and an active picnic takes priority until it finishes. Everyone sees the same NPC schedule and positions; quest progress and choices remain personal. No normal quest requires waiting for opening hours.
