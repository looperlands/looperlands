# LooperLands — Points Exchange context

This brief records the confirmed scope of the Points Exchange, not a new visual identity for the game.

Players spend game points at the main-map merchant beside the event board. Prices start at 250 points; changes to traits and avatar class preserve level and XP. Each of three daily challenges pays 1 point, with a 5-point bonus for completing challenges on four distinct days in a UTC week. Existing active-day and streak earnings continue. Fishing always requires 15 successful catches and harvesting always requires 20 harvests, regardless of level. Combat targets enemies within two levels of the daily avatar level, capped at level 100.

Keep the original game panels, fonts, palette and navigation. Improve their content with illustrated equipment and class tiles; do not add a scenic background, book skin or replacement theme. A centered equipment card and bounded trait spinner make rerolls readable. Use small golden sparks and the game's existing sound controls, with reduced-motion support; no beams.

Permanent titles, emotes and an optional Golden Spark Trail belong to the wallet. Equip or remove the trail through Collection. Event-exclusive emotes and future event visual styles remain earned rewards, never shop products.
